A few friendly pointers

↔

Give a window a wiggle. Drag its striped title bar, then let go. It springs right back. Works with a mouse or your finger.

▣

Make a little room. The square in each title bar minimizes a window. The bottom dock brings it back.

♫

Set the mood. Change the Spotify link or edit your notes. Changes stay in this browser; export preferences to update the site source.

⌨

Keyboard welcome. Tab moves between controls. Enter opens links. Escape closes menus and dialogs. Your reduced-motion setting is respected.
